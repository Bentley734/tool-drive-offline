TOOL DRIVE OFFLINE

Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or another modern browser.
Double-click the file, or right-click it and choose Open with your browser.
No installation, server, account, or internet connection is needed.
You can copy index.html anywhere; it contains the entire app and catalog.

FIND SOMETHING TO PLAY
Search for a date, venue, city, taper, or song. Set a minimum sound rating,
choose a year or recording type, and open a show to compare its sources.
The default view includes sources marked OTD (on the drive).
Copy a show's date, then search that date in your own Plexamp library.
This app does not connect to Plex or know which shows you own.

RATINGS AND DATA
Snapshot downloaded November 21, 2026 from the Tool Drive Project's public
show, source, song, and ordered-setlist metadata. Setlists originate from
setlist.fm. This is an independent personal offline browser.

Ratings measure recording SOUND QUALITY out of 10, not show performance.
A show's displayed score is its highest-rated source matching your filters.
An unrated source is displayed as unrated, never as a 0/10 score.
The public data does not include vote counts; no confidence ranking is used.
Ratings and catalog entries are frozen at the snapshot and do not update
automatically. Some setlists are partial; omitted items are indicated.

OTD = on the drive. Confirmed = exists, not yet acquired by the project.
Unconfirmed = believed to exist. Unknown = no known recording.
These describe the source archive, not the contents of your Plex library.

OFFLINE AND AUDIO-FREE
All app code and catalog data are embedded in index.html.
No audio files were downloaded or bundled. Streaming, download, and Google
Drive links have been removed. There are no external images, fonts, scripts,
analytics, or automatic network requests. The page also blocks connections
and media through its Content Security Policy.

Saved shows are stored only in this browser's local storage. They depend on
the browser and file location; moving the file or clearing browser data may
lose the saved list. If local storage is unavailable, saving lasts until the
page is closed.

OPTIONAL SOURCE FILES
The full project folder also contains app.template.html, catalog.json, and
build_catalog.py for inspection or rebuilding. They are not needed to use
index.html. With Python installed, build_catalog.py --refresh explicitly
downloads just the four public metadata files and rebuilds the page.
The rebuild requires internet access; ordinary app use never does.
