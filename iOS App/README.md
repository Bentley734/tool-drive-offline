# Tool Drive Offline for iPhone and iPad

Version 2.0.0.

Open **Tool Drive Offline.xcodeproj** in Xcode 26 or later on a Mac. Choose the **Tool Drive Offline** scheme, select an iPhone or iPad simulator running iOS 17 or later, and press **Run**. All sources, concert photography, icon layers, visualizers and the offline catalog are included in the standalone project download.

To try the prebuilt app in Simulator, unzip **Tool.Drive.Offline.iOS.Simulator.zip** and drag **Tool Drive Offline.app** onto an open iPhone or iPad simulator running iOS 17 or later.

To run on your own iPhone or iPad, connect the device to your Mac and select it in Xcode. In the app target’s **Signing & Capabilities** settings, choose your development team. Use a unique bundle identifier if your team needs one. Press **Run** and follow any pairing or Developer Mode prompts on the device. The separate **Unsigned.ipa** download requires signing before installation.

## Browse the archive

Search 1,299 documented shows by venue, city, date, song or recording details. The initial **On the drive** filter includes 759 shows with 1,342 available recording sources in the bundled November 21, 2026 catalog. Open **Filters** to include the full archive. A rating measures the sound quality of a recording on a ten-point scale.

On iPhone, tap a result to open its recordings and setlist. On iPad, the catalog and selected show share the screen when space permits. Tap the bookmark to keep a show in **Saved**. Open the palette in the catalog toolbar for **Appearance**.

## Appearance

Liquid Glass is the default on first launch. An existing theme choice takes precedence. Choose **Front Row**, **Night Shift**, **Graphite**, **Laser Grid**, or **Liquid Glass**. The selection carries through the catalog, recording details, Library and player. **Logo** switches between the Classic and 2006 wordmarks. Front Row offers **Blue Silhouette** and **Governors Ball**. Liquid Glass offers nine concert wallpapers and **Shuffle**, which picks a different photo the next time you launch into that theme.

**Liquid Glass controls** adds glass to controls in the four standard themes. Choosing the Liquid Glass theme enables the controls. Turning them off from that theme returns to your last standard theme. The app uses system glass on iOS and iPadOS 26 or later and translucent materials on earlier versions. Reduce Transparency gives the controls solid backgrounds.

## Listen on the go

- Open an available recording and tap **Stream**. Open the player for the track queue, playback position, EQ and visualizer.
- Choose **Source → Save stream offline** to keep a recording on the device. **Library** shows transfer progress and completed recordings.
- Choose **Library → Import audio from Files** to copy playable audio from your device or iCloud Drive. Select several tracks to keep them together. The app keeps each completed recording until you remove it from Library.
- Choose **Library → Plex music library** to connect your own account and choose a server and music library. From a recording, **Source → Find on Plex** searches for its date and source version.
- **Library → Refresh catalog** checks the Tool Drive Project for updated metadata. Browsing, bookmarks, wallpapers, imported audio and completed downloads work offline. Archive streams, fresh metadata, original-file links and Plex connections need an accessible server.

The app includes concert metadata and photos. Recording audio comes from your library or the archive when you choose to listen or save. See **Resources/NOTICE.md** for catalog credits.
