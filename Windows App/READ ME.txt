TOOL DRIVE OFFLINE - WINDOWS APP

Double-click Tool Drive Offline.exe to open the app.
No installation, administrator rights, browser, or local server is needed.
The entire show catalog is embedded in the executable. You can move the EXE
to any folder, or pin it using Windows' normal taskbar/Start controls.

This app uses .NET Framework 4.8, which is already installed on this PC.
Another computer must also have .NET Framework 4.8 or later installed.

FIND A SHOW
Search for a date, venue, city, taper, or song. Filter by sound rating,
recording type, year, and source status. Open a show for its recording
versions, lineage notes, and setlist. Copy its date and search in Plexamp
for your own recording. The app does not connect to Plex or inspect your
music library.

All ratings measure recording SOUND QUALITY out of 10. The score shown for
a show is the highest rating among sources matching ALL your filters.
Unrated recordings are not zero-rated. Vote counts are unavailable.

The default status is OTD (on the project's drive). Confirmed means a
recording exists but the project has not acquired it; Unconfirmed means
believed to exist; Unknown means no known recording. These labels do not
describe what is in your Plex library.

OFFLINE DATA
1,299 shows from the Tool Drive Project's public metadata, downloaded
November 21, 2026. Setlists originate from setlist.fm and may be partial.
This is an independent personal offline browser. Data and ratings are
frozen at that snapshot. No audio was downloaded or bundled, and there
are no streaming, download, Google Drive, or other external links.
The app makes no network requests and has no automatic updater.

SAVED SHOWS
Saved show IDs are stored in:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tool Drive Offline\saved-shows.json
They remain saved when you move or replace the EXE on the same Windows
account. This list is separate from the earlier HTML browser's saved list.
If the app cannot save the file, it reports that the list is session-only.

SOURCE AND REBUILD
The project folder contains the C# source, icon, manifest, and build-app.ps1.
Run build-app.ps1 in PowerShell to rebuild using the installed .NET Framework
compiler. It embeds the existing ../catalog.json; building needs no network.
